Every site in the proposal enrolled fewer than 400 students with building use under 65%, and none is projected to gain enrollment. The 14 elementary sites averaged 307 students and 51.3% occupancy in 2025–26; only Anderson and Dolores Huerta cleared 60%.

The list touches all seven school board districts and six of seven feeder patterns. Edison is the exception: every Edison school sat at 75% occupancy or above, two of them above 95%.

      School list

      The 17 closures and the building changes come from slide 27 of the TPS board presentation Superintendent's Proposal to Address Budget Deficit (14 elementary, 2 middle and 1 alternative site), presented at the 21 September 2026 board meeting.

      Addresses for all 85 mapped campuses: Tulsa Public Schools Directory of Schools, 2026–2027, the district's printable directory, cross-checked against individual school site footers on tulsaschools.org.

      Coordinates

      U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder, Public_AR_Current benchmark, one address at a time. Every point on this map is a geocoder response, not an estimate — with one exception: the 3100 block of W Edison St has no address range in Census TIGER, so the Central Middle / Central High campus uses the published coordinate from the English Wikipedia article Central High School (Tulsa, Oklahoma).

      Each campus is assigned to its census tract by point-in-polygon. Greenwood Leadership Academy, Central and S.O.A.R. fall in Osage County: west Tulsa north of the river lies across the county line.

      Income and boundaries

      Tract figures: ACS 2024 five-year, tables B19013 (median household income), B01003 (population) and B17020 (poverty status), from the Census Bureau API. Tract boundaries: Census cartographic boundary files (500k), 2022 vintage.

      ZIP figures: ACS 2024 five-year as republished by IncomeByZipCode.com. ZIP boundaries: 2010 Census ZCTAs. ZCTAs approximate postal ZIPs, so a few campuses sit a few metres across a line from the ZIP the district lists.

      Base map: CARTO Positron / Dark Matter vector tiles on OpenStreetMap data, rendered with MapLibre GL JS.

      Site detail and process

      Feeder pattern, enrollment, occupancy, bond-history and art/music staffing figures: Tulsa World, “18 schools listed in draft TPS closure, repurposing proposal,” 21 September 2026, drawing on TPS 2025–26 building-capacity and enrollment data and open records requests. The World's 18 is the 17 closure sites plus Central High School, which is repurposed rather than closed.

      Superintendent Ebony Johnson announced the consolidation review on 8 September 2026, seeking $12 million in cuts; board votes were scheduled for 21 September, 5 October and 19 October 2026 (Tulsa Flyer, 18 September 2026).
